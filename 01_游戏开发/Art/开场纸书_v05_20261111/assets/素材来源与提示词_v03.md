## 1. 图像与三维制作的边界

三张新纸卡图集均由 **内置 imagegen 工具**生成或进行风格编辑，未使用 CLI 图像 API。它们不是 Blender 原生建模后渲染出的图片。Blender 将图像按角色／建筑／植物区域引用，依据透明轮廓生成薄纸卡网格，再增加纸厚、支脚、分层距离、灯光和关键帧。书页、竖直书脊、扇形底台、工位与部分道具是 Blender 中实际存在的几何体。

角色在本片中是带贴图的纸卡轮廓，并非本片新完成的骨骼绑定角色。动画不应被描述为已完成 Unity 过场接入或角色游戏动作。

## 3. 提示词记录

### 3.1 双人纸卡 v01：可核对的原始生成提示词

调用方式：内置 `imagegen`，`transparent_background: true`。两张输入图分别是上节的纸团、印桃正式多视图，用于身份和造型参考。

以下内容可从本次会话中工具调用核对，为原始提示词原文；它对应 v01 双人图，不应误认为已包含后续扁平化编辑要求。

```text
Create production animation cutout asset, preserving exact character identities of the two supplied reference sheets. Image 1 is boy Zhituan design reference. Image 2 is girl Yintao design reference. A single wide landscape transparent PNG with exactly two full-body characters side by side, separated by generous clear space, no overlap. Boy on left: folded ivory paper hat, blue ribbon, moss green Song dynasty tunic, ivory apron, blue pocket, dark shorts, square large head. Girl on right: brown folded paper twin low buns with red ribbons, vermilion Song dynasty tunic, ivory trim, dark blue shorts, red square seal pendant at waist. Match faces and folded papercraft material exactly, small limbs, charming joyful expressions. Both stand facing slightly toward one another but mostly front, arms open in welcoming pose, empty hands, single clean cutout each, feet fully visible. Beautiful tangible layered paper edges, warm soft lighting, no floor, no cast shadow plane, no lettering, no labels, no other figures or objects, NO reference sheet grid. This pair will pop up as standing paper theatre characters in an antique Chinese storybook.
```

### 3.2 最终双人扁平剪纸：制作要求摘要

本段是已知制作要求的摘要，**不是该次风格编辑调用的逐字提示词**；本说明没有取得该次完整原始提示词，不能用摘要冒充原始记录。

保留纸团的纸帽、蓝色绑带、绿衣，以及印桃的红衣、双髻和红色发饰，维持两名角色的身份、配色与轮廓。将 v01 的立体纸偶质感编辑为正面、扁平、分层彩纸剪贴风格；透明背景，两名角色分开、全身可见，便于 Blender 各自引用。采用纸片切边与克制的纸纹，减少圆润雕塑体积和真实光照阴影，不加入文字或其他角色。

### 3.3 三件建筑剪纸图集：制作要求摘要

本段是制作要求摘要，**不是该次生成调用的逐字提示词**；本说明没有取得该次完整原始提示词。

全新生成一张透明背景建筑图集，包含三件互不重叠的独立古风纸艺建筑：店铺、交子作坊门脸、可用于交付窗口背景的店铺建筑。面向镜头的平面剪纸结构，黛青屋瓦、木构门窗、暖色纸墙，加入瓦片层次、窗棂、门框、布帘等清楚细节。各建筑保留完整外轮廓和透明间隔，便于按图集区域生成薄纸卡。画面用于宋代灵感的架空纸艺世界，不作为特定历史建筑的考据复原。

### 3.4 银杏、竹枝、如意云纹：最终提示词原文

调用方式：内置 `imagegen` 全新生成，`transparent_background: true`，无输入参考图，非 CLI。以下逐字引用同目录 `扁平纸艺植物云纹_v02_prompt.txt` 中的“最终生成提示词（原文）”部分。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a transparent PNG production asset sheet for a Chinese papercraft storybook animation.
Make ONE landscape 3:2 image containing exactly THREE COMPLETE SEPARATE OBJECTS arranged left to right with generous transparent gaps and no overlap: LEFT a golden ginkgo tree, CENTER a blue-green bamboo sprig, RIGHT an ivory Chinese ruyi-shaped curled cloud ornament.
This must be true TWO-DIMENSIONAL handmade colored-paper collage, viewed exactly straight on, flat silhouettes with crisp craft-knife-cut edges. Emphasize delicate cardstock fibers, fine scored fold lines, cut-paper negative space, and only two or three thin layers of flat paper with extremely subtle contact shadows. The visual language is elegant traditional Chinese paper craft, warm and refined, beautifully detailed but uncluttered.
The ginkgo tree has a flat angular warm-brown cut-paper trunk and branches, a golden yellow canopy made from distinct fan-shaped leaves with fine fan-shaped engraved/scored lines. Do not give the trunk cylindrical volume. The bamboo sprig is muted blue-green, with flat slender segmented stems and sharply pointed diamond-like paper leaves, clean vein scoring. The ivory ruyi cloud is a decorative curled cloud silhouette, with carefully carved internal cut-out curves and open negative-space contours. Cloud remains flat; it is not a white fluffy volume.
Each of the three objects is fully visible including the whole tree trunk base, bamboo stem ends and entire cloud contour. They are independent cutouts, not part of one scene. Transparent alpha all around each object AND between all three objects. No paper backing rectangle, no scenery ground, no border, no checkerboard printed in the artwork.
ABSOLUTE STYLE EXCLUSIONS: no thick 3D rounded trunk, no sculpted round forms, no clay, no toy-like plastic, no realistic lighting, no volumetric rendering, no realistic shading, no glossy highlights, no bevelled thick edges, no photorealism, no depth-of-field blur. No text, letters, symbols, labels, watermark, numbers, people or animals. Keep the overall objects deliberately planar and preserve refined paper material quality.
```

## 4. 音乐与声音

最终音轨为 `开场配乐与纸声_v02.wav`，来源于本项目脚本 `01_游戏开发/Scripts/开场纸书_v03_20261111/create_audio.py` 的原创合成流程；最终 WAV 与 v02 assets 的同名修正版逐字一致。详细机器可读参数见 `开场音轨来源_v02.json`。

- 48 秒；44.1 kHz；16-bit；立体声。
- D、E、F♯、A、B 五声音阶的拨弦合成；纸声为滤波噪声合成，落印为合成的木章触碰音。
- 开场纸声在 0.2 秒；五次转页声修正为 **6.7、14.7、22.7、30.7、38.7 秒**，对应竖立 carousel 的转页段。
- 第 29 秒一次落印声；无旁白；未使用外部音乐、采样库或参考视频原声。
- JSON 记录峰值约 −6.98 dBFS、RMS 约 −25.00 dBFS；这些是音频生成记录，不代替最终成片的听音检查。
